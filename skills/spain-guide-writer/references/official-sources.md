# Official sources by topic

Start here, then follow links within these sites to the specific page. Always cite the exact page you used, not just the home page, and confirm the page is current. If a domain below has changed or a page has moved, find the new official page; don't fall back to an unofficial summary.

## Spain: national

| Topic | Where to check |
|---|---|
| Laws, royal decrees, official notices (the legal text itself) | BOE, Boletín Oficial del Estado: boe.es |
| General government procedures, appointments, Cl@ve | Punto de Acceso General: administracion.gob.es; Cl@ve: clave.gob.es |
| Residency, TIE, NIE, visas, Withdrawal Agreement residents | Ministerio de Inclusión, Seguridad Social y Migraciones, immigration portal (extranjeros.inclusion.gob.es); Policía Nacional (policia.es) for fingerprinting and TIE appointments; Spanish consulates (exteriores.gob.es) for visas applied for from the UK |
| Tax: income tax (IRPF), wealth tax, non-resident tax, Modelo 720/721, tax residency | Agencia Tributaria: agenciatributaria.es and its sede electrónica |
| Social security, healthcare entitlement, S1, pensions in Spain | Seguridad Social: seg-social.es |
| Public healthcare (national framework) | Ministerio de Sanidad: sanidad.gob.es (services themselves are run by each region; see below) |
| Driving licences, exchange, fines, ITV | DGT, Dirección General de Tráfico: dgt.es |
| Wills, inheritance procedure, powers of attorney | Consejo General del Notariado: notariado.org; Registro de Últimas Voluntades, via the Ministerio de Justicia (mjusticia.gob.es) |
| Property registration | Registradores de España: registradores.org |
| Consumer issues and scams | Policía Nacional (policia.es), Guardia Civil (guardiacivil.es), INCIBE (incibe.es) for online fraud |

## Other regions

Each autonomous community has its own government site and tax agency. Search for "[community] agencia tributaria" or "[community] sede electrónica", and confirm you're on the official .es / .gob.es / regional domain. This matters most for inheritance and gift tax, the regional part of income tax, property transfer tax (ITP), healthcare registration and housing rules. When rules vary, say so and point readers to their own region rather than listing all 17.

## Local

The ayuntamiento (town hall) handles the padrón (empadronamiento), local taxes (IBI, rubbish) and local permits. Procedures vary by town, so describe the general process and tell readers to check their own town hall's website or office.

## How to cite

For each source, record: page title · publisher · URL · date checked (and "last updated" if the page shows it). In the guide, list sources under **Official sources** with a short note on what each one confirms.
