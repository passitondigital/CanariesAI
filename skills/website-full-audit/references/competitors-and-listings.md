# Competitors and listings

Both need web search. If you have none, skip these checks, say so in "What I couldn't check", and score area 4 only on what the website itself shows (e.g. whether it links to a Google profile), clearly marked as partial.

## Competitor comparison

**Choose 2–3 competitors.** Use the ones the client named. If none, search as a customer would ("[service] in [town]", "best [service] near [town]") and take the local businesses that appear at the top. Leave out national chains unless the client names one.

**For each, look at:** the homepage headline, Google rating and review count, whether prices are shown, whether there's an FAQ, online booking, and structured data (only if you can fetch their HTML).

**Optionally ask an AI-style question.** Run a search the way a customer might ask an assistant ("who's the best [service] in [town]?") and note who appears. Be clear this is a snapshot, not a ranking.

**Report as a table plus lessons:**

| | Client | Competitor A | Competitor B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clear "what + where" headline | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Google rating (reviews) | 4.8 (23) | 4.6 (140) | 4.9 (61) |
| Prices shown | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ |
| FAQ page | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Online booking or ordering | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |

Then give 3–4 short "what to learn" points. Keep the tone fair and factual: never disparage competitors, and focus on what the client can do. Only state what you actually saw, with the date checked.

## Google Business Profile and directory listings

**Google Business Profile.** Search for the business name and town. From the results (the knowledge panel or Maps listing), note: does it exist; category; rating and review count; hours; website link; whether the owner replies to reviews; how recent the photos and posts are. You often can't see everything without logging in, so record only what's visible and suggest the owner checks the rest in their profile dashboard.

**Directories.** Check the 2–4 that matter for the trade:
- Trades: Checkatrade, TrustATrader, MyBuilder, Rated People, Yell
- Hospitality: TripAdvisor, Google, OpenTable, Yell
- Health and beauty: Treatwell, Fresha, NHS directory, professional registers (HCPC, GOsC, GDC)
- Professional services: Yell, professional body directories, Bark
- Everyone: Yell, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook

**Consistency (NAP: name, address, phone).** Put each listing's name, address and phone side by side with the website's. Flag any differences, even small ones ("Bramble Bakery Ltd" vs "Bramble Bakery"; old phone numbers; an old address). Consistent details help Google and AI tools trust that they're all the same business.
